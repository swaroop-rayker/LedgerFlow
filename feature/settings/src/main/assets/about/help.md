## Where is my data kept?

On this phone, in an encrypted database. LedgerFlow has no account, no server and no cloud copy. Backups go only to a folder you choose.

## Does LedgerFlow send anything over the internet?

No. Reading messages and receipts and every chart are worked out on the phone. The app declares the Internet permission only because Google's on-phone text reader brings it; Data permissions under Security & privacy explains each permission.

## Why does nothing reach my ledger until I tap it?

So nothing is added that you have not seen. Payments LedgerFlow reads from notifications, messages and receipts wait in the Inbox until you approve them, and you can change any detail first.

## Why was a payment not picked up?

Check that notification access is on (Settings > Notifications) and that the app the payment came from is ticked under Payment apps. Some payments send no notification or message at all; add those yourself with the + button.

## My bank sent a message and a notification for one payment. Why is there only one entry?

LedgerFlow recognises the same payment arriving twice and keeps one. The other is not deleted: it is under the Inbox's Suppressed filter.

## What is the 24-word recovery phrase for?

It is the only way to open your backups, and to get your data back on a new or reset phone. LedgerFlow never stores it and cannot show it again, so keep it written down somewhere safe.

## What if I lose my recovery phrase?

While this phone works, your data stays usable. But a backup cannot be opened without the phrase, and nobody, including LedgerFlow, can recover it. Make a new backup and keep the phrase safe.

## How do I back up and restore?

Back up now writes an encrypted backup to the folder you chose; nightly backups can run on their own. To restore, install LedgerFlow on the new phone and choose "Restore from a backup" on the first screen, then type the 24 words.

## Why are spending and income never added together?

They are two separate books. Netting them would hide how much you spent behind how much arrived, so every total, chart and budget is one book only.

## What does App lock protect?

It asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock before LedgerFlow's screens show. It hides the screens; your data is encrypted whether it is on or off, and the app keeps capturing payments while locked.

## What does Hide amounts on Home do?

It shows dots in place of every amount on Home, for when someone can see your screen. One tap shows them; they hide again when you leave Home. Other screens show amounts as usual.
