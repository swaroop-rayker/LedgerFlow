## Since 0.1.0

### A new Home
- Home shows the month so far: what you spent and what you received, each on its own line and never added together, beside the same days of last month.
- Your budgets closest to their limit, the charges expected before the month ends, and your newest entries in each book.
- Hide amounts on Home: dots in place of every amount until you tap one.

### A new look
- A new design, dark by default. Choose System, Light or Dark under Settings > Appearance, and how much the app moves.
- Profile is the fourth tab. Settings lives inside it, with a page for each section.

### Settings
- Currency & region: how amounts are grouped (lakh or western) and the order dates are written in.
- Notifications: Android's budget-alert switch, and a warning when your spending is on course to pass a budget before the period ends.
- Payment apps: choose whose notifications LedgerFlow reads, and add an app of your own.
- Security & privacy: App lock with your fingerprint, face or screen lock; block screenshots; and Data permissions, which shows everything LedgerFlow may use and why.
- About: this page, Help, and how to report a bug.

### Fixes
- Messages that are not payments (one-time codes, balance alerts, offers) are kept under "Not transactions" rather than filling your Inbox.
- Switching tabs no longer shows two screens at once.
- On a phone set to a right-to-left language, text, names and amounts sit where they should.
