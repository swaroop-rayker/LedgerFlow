These terms cover your use of LedgerFlow, a personal expense tracker that runs on your phone.

## Using LedgerFlow

LedgerFlow is provided free of charge, for your own personal record keeping. You may use it as you wish, for yourself.

## Your data is yours

Everything you put into LedgerFlow stays on your phone and belongs to you. LedgerFlow has no account and no server, and holds no copy of your data anywhere. The privacy policy describes this in full.

## Your recovery phrase and backups

Your backups can be opened only with the 24-word recovery phrase you write down at setup. LedgerFlow cannot see, store or recover it. Keeping the phrase safe, and making backups, are your responsibility. Without the phrase, a backup cannot be restored.

## Not financial advice

LedgerFlow records and summarises what you spend and receive. Its figures, budgets and warnings are records and estimates, not financial, tax or legal advice. Check anything important against your bank's own statements.

## Read from messages, approved by you

LedgerFlow suggests entries from the messages and notifications it reads, and nothing reaches your ledger until you approve it. A suggestion can be wrong; it is yours to check before you approve.
